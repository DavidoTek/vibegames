WebDAW

A small loop-based DAW. Every track loops over the song length (Bars). Pick a track in the lane list, then draw notes in the editor or play them on the keyboard.

  • Space play / stop · R record · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo
  • A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P play notes (laid out like a piano), Z / X octave down / up
  • On drum tracks the home row A S D F G H J K L ; plays the pads.
  • Editor: click or tap an empty cell to add a note, tap a note to delete it (right-click works too), drag a note to move it, drag its right edge to change the length, Shift+drag up/down for velocity. Drag empty space or use the wheel to scroll, Ctrl+wheel to zoom.
  • Recording: arm ●, start playback and play the keyboard; notes snap to the grid.
  • Your song is saved in this browser automatically. Save / Open move it as a file, WAV renders two loops to audio.
